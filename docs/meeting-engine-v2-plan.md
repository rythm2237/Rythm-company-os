# Meeting Engine V2 — Human participation, language preference, and executive summaries

## Product decisions

1. Human CEO is always invited to governed multi-agent meetings, but participation is optional and non-blocking.
2. During a running meeting, the Human CEO may add a contribution to the persistent transcript. Subsequent agent turns see it as part of meeting context.
3. Meeting language is a preference, not a platform limitation. English, Persian, or any other language can be selected when preparing the session. Agents respond in the selected language unless the meeting context requires otherwise.
4. Long meetings expose an on-demand Executive Summary control. Summary output must include key points, consensus, material disagreements, risks, decision-ready options, and next steps. Full transcript remains available and expandable.
5. Human CEO remains final decision authority. Summaries and agent recommendations are advisory only.
6. External actions remain disabled and separately approval-gated.
