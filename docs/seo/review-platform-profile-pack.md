## Neutral short description

RYTHM Company OS is a governed AI workforce platform for building and operating specialized AI Agent teams under Human CEO authority. It combines role-based AI Agents, Company Memory, structured meetings, approvals, and traceable execution in one operating environment.

## Neutral long description

RYTHM Company OS is a governed AI workforce platform designed for founders, operators, and organizations that want to use specialized AI Agent teams with explicit roles, shared Company Memory, structured collaboration, approval controls, and traceable execution. The Human CEO retains final authority over consequential actions. RYTHM is currently available as a Public Beta and provides public product, pricing, trust, security, AI transparency, integration, and demo pages that can be independently verified.
