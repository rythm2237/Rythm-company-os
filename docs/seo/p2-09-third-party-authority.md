# P2-09 — Third-Party Authority Execution

Checkpoint: 2026-09-03

## Acceptance boundary

P2-09 closes only when unaffiliated external publishers or software-discovery platforms publish substantive, verifiable RYTHM mentions. Self-published RYTHM pages, founder-authored posts, GitHub, and the RYTHM LinkedIn company page do not count as third-party coverage.

## Current external-search baseline

A fresh web search on 2026-09-03 found no verified independent substantive coverage for `RYTHM Company OS`, `rythm-os.com`, or founder + RYTHM combinations. Search results were dominated by RYTHM-owned pages and cached legacy-host pages. Therefore the baseline remains zero independently verified substantive mentions.

## Submission targets

### Product Hunt
- Purpose: public product launch and community discovery.
- Account dependency: requires a personal Product Hunt account; company accounts cannot post.
- Current process: submit product URL through the maker posting flow; launch can be scheduled.
- Evidence required for credit: live public Product Hunt product page.

### AlternativeTo
- Purpose: independent software-discovery/alternatives listing.
- Account dependency: verified user account required.
- Current process: Suggest new application, provide platform/license/description/tags, then editorial review.
- Evidence required for credit: approved public RYTHM application page.

### SaaSHub
- Purpose: independent software marketplace / alternatives listing.
- Current process: submit product, list categories and competitors, verify with domain email where possible, pass editorial approval.
- Evidence required for credit: approved public RYTHM product page.

### Futurepedia
- Purpose: AI-tool directory visibility.
- Current state checked 2026-09-03: official submission page currently presents paid listing tiers and editorial approval.
- Decision: do not spend or submit without explicit founder approval.
- Evidence required for credit: approved public listing with disclosure of any paid placement.
