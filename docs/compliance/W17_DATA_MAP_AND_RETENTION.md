# W17 | Internal data map and retention matrix

Owner: Tayyebialashti Yaser E.V. / RYTHM Privacy Operations. Source baseline: main at f27cad51496d3d72e797a25342d2f33375644c60. The retention targets below come from the existing GDPR operational baseline. Technical enforcement and provider-specific timeframes require evidence. Read with [GDPR operational baseline](GDPR_OPERATIONAL_BASELINE.md) and [legal review register](W17_LEGAL_REVIEW.md).

| Data and subjects | Source and destinations | Access, erasure and retention disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account, authentication, organization memberships, preferences | Supabase Auth, `customer_profiles`, `organization_members`, `user_ai_preferences`. Vercel handles application requests. | Match verified subject ID and membership separately. Preserve other memberships and controller data when deleting an account. Active service plus 30-day operational deletion target after validated instruction, subject to documented exception. Auth and session revocation need staging evidence. |
| Customers, leads, CRM contacts, workspace participants, documents | `crm_contacts`, customer and contract records, `project_context_documents`, meeting participants and messages, private Supabase Storage. | Customer controller establishes authority and scope. Review free text for other persons and preserve statutory records. Active workspace plus 30-day operational target for eligible active records after validated instruction. |
| Agents, user-authored prompts, inference, memories and generated files | `agent_runs`, `company_memory`, `agent_attachments`, `agent_artifacts`, `company_knowledge_chunks`, `project_decision_memory`, `aiw_messages`, `aiw_memories`, `aiw_saved_prompts`, `aiw_files`, `aiw_file_chunks`. OpenAI receives context through invoked AI features. | Follow source retention and trace derived files, embeddings or chunks, memory, generated outputs and queued writes by scope. Remove binaries through Storage APIs once approved. Verify provider terms and identifier/deletion capability. Existing export covers selected structured tables. |
| Billing and account contacts | `billing_accounts`, `finance_invoices`, `assisted_billing_orders`, associated contracts and records. | Establish statutory period by document type. Isolate minimum eligible retained content and document the basis before cascading tenant deletion. |
| Audit, incidents, usage and provider logs | `audit_events`, `crm_audit_log`, `ai_usage_requests`, `usage_ledger`, Vercel, Supabase and Cloudflare operational metadata. | Existing targets are 90 days for ordinary operational logs and review at 12 months after termination for governance records. Incident and legal exceptions require scope, owner, basis and review date. Verify actual log sinks and purge schedules. |
| Privacy case and evidence | `privacy_requests`, dedicated privacy mailbox and operator records. | Retain minimal identity and decision evidence, never a full export for proof alone. Existing review target is three years. W17 limits requester writes to intake fields and denies read access to internal notes. |
| Backup copies | Supabase backups and W09 encrypted recovery artifacts. | Confirm each copy, owner, rotation/expiry and access. Keep deletion reconciliation metadata through the restore window and reapply eligible deletions before reconnecting restored systems. Verify dates and recovery procedure. |
| Consent, integrations and public channels | `rythm_analytics_consent`, Google Analytics if consented and configured, public intake, Cloudflare mail routing, customer-authorized integrations. | Browser/device consent is scoped to the browser. Confirm consent change propagation, analytics identifiers and retention, mail retention and each enabled integration's actual provider and contract. |
| Additional app surfaces | 2nya customer bookings and push subscriptions, public submissions, support access, guest AI sessions. | Determine controller relationship per surface, inventory configured providers and track expiration/deletion independently of workspace erase. |

The public [privacy notice](../../app/(public)/privacy/page.tsx), [DPA](../../app/(public)/dpa/page.tsx) and [subprocessor register](../../app/(public)/subprocessors/page.tsx) are contract and disclosure inputs. Treat their promises as review items. This source map still requires reconciliation against the active schema, bucket metadata, feature flags, job queues, provider settings and backup copies. A database foreign key or deletion of a Storage metadata row cannot prove the underlying binary was erased.
